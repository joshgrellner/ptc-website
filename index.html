About Penley Transportation

Oklahoma's Leading Crude Oil Hauling Company

Since 2013, Penley Transportation Company has been the trusted crude hauler for oil and gas companies across Oklahoma, Kansas, Texas, and New Mexico. Our professional crude oil trucking services connect oil fields to refineries throughout the heartland of America's energy sector.

As a family-owned business, we understand the importance of relationships built on trust, reliability, and personal service. Our commitment to safety isn't just a policy – it's a promise to our drivers, customers, and communities.
